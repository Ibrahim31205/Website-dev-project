Our Heritage

Forty Years of Quiet Craft on Manjanakkara Street.

Built on personal relationships, breathable textiles, and careful tailoring—MILAN has grown alongside generations of men in Cumbum.

Who We Are

Honest Cloth, Careful Fit, Everyday Value.

Run by proprietor Shajakhan A, MILAN Fabrics, Textiles & Tailoring has stood on Manjanakkara Street for over four decades. While menswear trends have shifted—from classic everyday cottons to Chinese-collared linen shirts, textured popcorn weaves, and light jersey activewear—our promise has stayed steady.

Every fabric bolt and readymade rack is selected for real South Indian weather: cool on the skin, durable through regular washes, and tailored to sit comfortably on every build.

Our Tailoring Process

From Bolt of Cloth to Finished Garment

Step One

Feel the Fabric First

Browse linen, linen cotton, Oxford cotton, cool cotton, blended cotton, Lycra, synthetic, and popcorn weaves across plain, striped, and printed finishes.

Step Two

Choose Your Cut & Collar

Pick regular-fit formal shirts, Chinese-collared shirts, traditional kurtas, or tailored trousers—measured personally for how you like your clothes to drape.

Step Three

Stitched by Our Own Team

Our dedicated in-house tailors handle cutting, stitching, and finishing under the same roof, keeping quality consistent and adjustments quick.
