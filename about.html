Our Story

Forty Years of Quiet Craft in Cumbum.

What began over four decades ago as a neighbourhood textile and tailoring counter has grown into a complete men’s clothing store trusted by families across generations.

Who We Are

Honest Cloth, Careful Stitching, Everyday Ease.

Led by proprietor Shajakhan A, MILAN Fabrics, Textiles & Tailoring stands on Manjanakkara Street in the heart of Cumbum. Over the past 40+ years, menswear has shifted from classic starched cottons to breathable linens, Chinese collars, Lycra stretches, popcorn textures, and easy jersey activewear—and our shelves have grown right alongside our customers.

Our promise stays simple: help every customer leave in clothing that breathes comfortably in Tamil Nadu weather, holds its shape wash after wash, and feels naturally tailored to them.

How Tailoring Works Here

From Unstitched Cloth to Finished Garment

Feel the Fabric

Browse linen, linen cotton, Oxford cotton, cool cotton, blended cotton, Lycra, synthetic, and popcorn weaves in plain, striped, or printed patterns.
